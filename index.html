Deal a hand to see the odds.

Rules: 6-deck shoe, dealer stands on soft 17, dealer checks for blackjack, blackjack pays 3:2, double on first two cards only, no splitting or surrender. Odds are computed exactly with an infinite-deck model (each rank drawn with fixed probability), which is the standard approximation for a multi-deck shoe. Expected value (EV) is per $100 bet: +10% means you win $10 per hand on average.
